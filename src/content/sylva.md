## Context

SYLVA AG developed an integrated platform for educators to create, teach, test, grade, and manage courses.
My role was Technical Lead from 2017 to 2024.

## My contribution

My responsibilities combined software architecture and design with hands-on development.
I worked on code reviews, project planning and estimation, team leadership, and technical guidance.
This brought implementation work and technical leadership into the same role, across the platform's educational workflows.

## Implementation

The technologies used in this work included Angular, Vue, and QuasarUI for application interfaces, alongside Docker, Loopback API, and MongoDB.
The project technology record also includes BigQuery, Google Cloud, Mathematica, and Wolfram Engine.

## What this work demonstrates

This experience reflects a sustained combination of technical leadership and software delivery.
The contribution spans planning, design, review, and development within an educational platform, rather than a single isolated feature.
